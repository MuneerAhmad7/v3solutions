Defensive Disclosure Policy

Security & Responsible Disclosure

We welcome constructive, ethical reports from security researchers that help identify weaknesses in our publicly accessible digital infrastructure.

At V3 Solutions, defensive engineering is woven into our software architectures, server configurations, and deployment pipelines. We welcome constructive, ethical reports from security researchers that help identify weaknesses in our publicly accessible digital infrastructure.

1. Authorized & Responsible Testing Guidelines

Security research conducted against V3 Solutions infrastructure must adhere strictly to the following parameters:

  • Do not access, modify, or destroy client data or user records.
  • Do not disrupt operational availability (denial-of-service, brute force flooding, resource exhaustion).
  • Do not execute social engineering, phishing, or physical intrusion attempts against staff or facilities.
  • Report discovered vulnerabilities promptly to our designated security email without public disclosure before remediation.

2. Scope

The following targets are in-scope for responsible disclosure:

*.v3solutions.dev (publicly facing primary corporate domains)

Third-party SaaS providers, payment gateways, and client infrastructure managed by clients independently are excluded from this program. Testing client production systems without prior written client authorization is strictly prohibited.

3. Required Information in Reports

  • Vulnerability type and potential business/security impact.
  • Target URL, parameter, or component affected.
  • Clear step-by-step reproduction instructions or non-destructive proof-of-concept.
  • Recommended mitigation or configuration adjustment.

4. Information That Must Never Be Submitted

Do not attach production passwords, private cryptographic keys, complete un-redacted client database dumps, or weaponized exploit binaries. Redact sensitive values prior to transmission.

5. Expected Response Process & Safe Harbor

When a report is received at security@v3solutions.dev:

  • Our security team will acknowledge receipt within 48 business hours.
  • We will evaluate the finding, determine severity, and communicate expected triage milestones.
  • Once remediated, we will notify you and credit your responsible disclosure.

We pledge not to pursue legal action against security researchers who conduct research in good faith adhering to these guidelines.

6. Dedicated Security Contact

Primary Security Inbox: security@v3solutions.dev
Emergency Escalation: Available for active incident triage for existing retainer clients.
